# Iwana Labs

We build and operate production AI systems.

We are a boutique AI consultancy in Madrid. We specialise in what happens after the demo. The output has to be right every time, the cost shows up on a budget, and a change six months later must not silently break what shipped.

## What we build

- Agents and automation
- Personalisation and ranking
- Search systems
- Content generation at scale
- Data and entity resolution
- Rescue and stabilisation of systems that have stopped being safe to change

## How we work

- **Two-week loops.** Each one ends in working software.
- **Full handover.** Modular, documented, yours to evolve.
- **No middle layer.** You work with the engineers who build it.

Every engagement runs the same five stages: 01 Intake · 02 Build · 03 Evaluate · 04 Ship · 05 Operate.

## Work with us

Have a system that needs to work in production? Tell us what you are building, or what has stopped working. Thirty minutes, no pitch deck.
